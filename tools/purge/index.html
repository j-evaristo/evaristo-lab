V = π/4 × d² × h of standing water, summed over cased + screened/open segments. US: gal = 0.0408 × d(in)² × h(ft). SI: L = 0.000785 × d(mm)² × h(m). DTW from the measuring point is corrected to below-land-surface using the stickup. Filter-pack porosity excluded (standard casing-volume convention). Sample at low flow (≤0.4–1 L/min) after purging; never let the pump cavitate for gas-sensitive analytes. Low-yield wells that dewater before 3 volumes: evacuate once, allow recovery, sample fresh inflow — or use low-flow sampling with field-parameter stabilization.
