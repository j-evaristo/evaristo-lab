Rainproof Street

You manage one city block: 48 tiles of 10 × 10 m. Rebuild it with a limited budget, then run a year of weather. Keep the streets dry, the sewer from overflowing, the summer bearable and, in Amsterdam, the wooden piles under the houses wet.

Choose a city

1. Pick a measure on the right.2. Click tiles to build (click again to remove).3. Run the year and read the report.
